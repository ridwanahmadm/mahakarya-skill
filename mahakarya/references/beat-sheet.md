# Beat-sheet transposition

A reference site is a sequence of **beats**: screens that each do one job with one composition and one motion. Copying the pixels gives a clone; copying the beats gives the same feeling with the client's own content.

## Step A: decompose the reference

For each beat, one row:

| # | Job (what the visitor learns or does) | Composition | Type | Motion | Material it needs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who and what, in one breath | Inset full-bleed photo in a rounded frame, floating pill nav, headline bottom-left in two lines (second line in the accent) | Condensed heavy display ≈ 8–9 vw, body 16–18 | An icon swaps in place of the first word; on scroll the photo shrinks into a centred card | One hero photograph that *is* the company |

Also record the **grammar**, the rules that repeat across beats:

- Canvas and surfaces (e.g. near-black field, one raised surface, no borders)
- The one accent and where it appears (CTA pills, the second headline line, selected states, data badges)
- Type roles and ratios (display : head : body : label, e.g. 9 : 4 : 1.1 : 0.8 vw at 1440)
- Label voice (e.g. small mono labels that say what the content is, often "Example …")
- Radius family (e.g. 24 media, 999 pills)
- Motion vocabulary (sticky stage, horizontal track with tilted cards, crossfade story with a progress rail, marquee, count-up badge)
- Chrome (floating nav, a persistent bottom-left chip, a footer card under an outline marquee)

Never record the reference's words except to name a beat.

## Step B: transpose

| Ref beat | Client beat | Ladder rung | Client material | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hero: "Four lines. One flow." | Hero: the company's own two-line promise | Who / what | Client photo X | real |
| Logo marquee | Marquee of the client's own project names | Proof of scale | CMS names | real |
| Testimonial quote | The company's written principle, attributed to the company | Trust | Approved copy | real |
| Metric row | Derived counts + placeholders | Scale | CMS + content module | mixed |

Rules:

- A reference beat whose material the client lacks (partner logos, testimonials, metrics) is **replaced by a beat that uses what the client has** (own project names, own principles, derived counts), not faked.
- Keep the reference's *pacing*: alternate dense and calm beats, photo-led and type-led beats, never two photo-led beats in a row unless the reference does it as one story.
- Rename everything: section names, labels and CTAs come from the client's domain language.
- Change at least one composition decision per beat from the reference (column split, crop, alignment, card count) so no screen is a trace.

## Step C: check

Screenshot each built beat beside its reference frame. Ask: same craft level? same rhythm? does any screen read as the reference with a new logo? Fix the last one first.
